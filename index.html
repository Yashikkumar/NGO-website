About Us

Ekta Mission is a non-profit organization deeply committed to uplifting the marginalized and bringing communities together. We believe in serving humanity indiscriminately, ensuring that compassion reaches those who need it most, regardless of caste, creed, or religion.

Our Work

Here is how we are making a difference in the community every single day.

🍽️ Food Distribution

Providing warm, nutritious meals to the needy and organizing regular community kitchens to fight hunger.

🏥 Healthcare

Running free medical camps, distributing essential medicines, and spreading vital health awareness.
