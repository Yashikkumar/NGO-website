About Ekta Mission

Ekta Mission is a registered non-profit welfare trust deeply committed to uplifting the marginalized and bringing communities together.

Founded on the principle that humanity comes first, we operate beyond caste, creed, or religion. Our dedicated volunteers work on the front lines every day to ensure that compassion reaches those who have been forgotten.

Our Core Initiatives

We work tirelessly to bridge the gap between those who want to help and those who desperately need it.

🍲

Zero Hunger Initiative

Through daily food drives and community kitchens, we ensure that nutritious meals reach daily wage workers, homeless individuals, and struggling families.

🩺

Accessible Healthcare

We organize free diagnostic camps, distribute essential medications, and provide maternal health awareness to underserved rural and urban communities.

🕊️

Community Unity

Beyond physical aid, we foster brotherhood and unity by organizing community events that transcend caste, religion, and social boundaries.
